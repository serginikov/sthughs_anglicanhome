Who We Are

Welcome to St Hugh's Home

St Hugh's Home is a charitable institution of the Anglican Diocese of Mauritius. We are a warm residential care home providing compassionate, long-term care for our elderly community welcoming residents aged 60 and over in a loving and supportive environment.

Ideally located in Rose Hill, we have a beautiful garden, easy access to public transport and local amenities. We welcome both private and government-supported residents, offering a safe, dignified, and fulfilling home.

Since 2010, we proudly uphold the Residential Care Home Act and the Protection for the Elderly Act, ensuring the highest standards of care, safety, and transparency for every resident and their family.

Our Story

A Rich History of Service

From humble beginnings sheltering orphans and vulnerable women, St Hugh's has evolved over decades into a leading residential care home for the elderly in Mauritius.

1900s
Anglican Diocese Shelters
The Anglican Diocese ran shelters for orphans, children from poor families and single women. In spite of limited funds, the Diocese kept these shelters running to serve the most vulnerable in the community.
1960
Relocation After Cyclone Carol
After Cyclone Carol, the boys' orphanage was relocated to Ambrose Street at the former St Paul's Theological College. Girls and women were sheltered at the Rose Belle orphanage.
1975
Cyclone Gervaise and Consolidation
Cyclone Gervaise destroyed the Rose Belle orphanage building. The women moved to Rose Hill to share the girls' block. The Rachel and Evelyn Blocks were later constructed, named after two devoted ladies.
1980s
Focus Shifts to the Elderly
As demand for elderly shelter grew, the Diocese decided to convert existing buildings into residential blocks for paying residents. Rainbow Nursery School was also established in 1980 to serve working-class families.
2010
Marthe and Mary Block
In June 2010, during the episcopacy of Archbishop Ian Ernest, four self-care flats were built. On 5th July 2014 they were named Marthe and Mary by Mrs Caroline Welby, wife of Archbishop Justin Welby of Canterbury.

What We Offer

Our Care Services

We offer a comprehensive range of services tailored to the individual needs of each resident, ensuring a comfortable and fulfilling life.

Residential Long-Term Care

Full-time residential care in a warm, home-like environment with 24/7 professional support for up to 56 residents.

Personal Care Assistance

Daily living support including bathing, dressing, grooming, and mobility assistance delivered with dignity and respect.

Nutritional Meals

Balanced, nutritious meals prepared daily with attention to individual dietary requirements and preferences.

Spiritual & Pastoral Care

Regular worship services, pastoral visits, and spiritual support aligned with Anglican Christian principles and values.

Social & Recreational Activities

Engaging social programmes, recreational activities, and community events to nurture mental and emotional wellbeing.
